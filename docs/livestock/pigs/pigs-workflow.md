# Pigs Workflow
# Main stages


## 🐖 Main Stages of Pig Production

### 1. Breed Selection
Selection of pig breeds according to meat production objectives, growth performance, feed efficiency, reproductive characteristics and farm conditions.

### 2. Breeding
Selection of breeding animals, mating or artificial insemination, and management of the breeding herd.

### 3. Pregnancy (Gestation)
Monitoring pregnant sows throughout gestation, managing nutrition and health, and preparing suitable conditions for farrowing.

### 4. Farrowing
Birth of piglets, supervision of the farrowing process, and immediate post-farrowing care of the sow and newborn piglets.

### 5. Piglet Care
Initial care of newborn piglets, colostrum intake, temperature management, health monitoring and early nutrition.

### 6. Weaning
Transition of piglets from sow's milk to solid feed, including weaning management, stress reduction and health monitoring.

### 7. Nursery
Management of weaned piglets, including specialized feeding, suitable housing, temperature control and early growth monitoring.

### 8. Growing
Development of young pigs through balanced nutrition, appropriate housing and regular monitoring of growth performance.

### 9. Feeding and Nutrition
Providing balanced feed according to the animals' growth stages, nutritional requirements and production objectives. Monitoring feed consumption and feed conversion ratio (FCR).

### 10. Fattening (Finishing)
Final production stage focused on achieving the required market weight, improving feed efficiency and meeting meat production standards.

### 11. Weight Monitoring
Regular measurement of body weight, average daily gain (ADG) and feed conversion ratio to evaluate growth performance and determine market readiness.

### 17. Sales
Marketing and sale of live pigs, pork carcasses, fresh meat and processed pork products through appropriate distribution channels.
