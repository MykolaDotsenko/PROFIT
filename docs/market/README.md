# Market

Market-facing business documentation lives here.

## Go-to-Market
Document:
- pricing and packaging;
- monetization;
- sales strategy;
- onboarding;
- partnerships;
- launch planning;
- acquisition and retention;
- SEO and content strategy.

## Competitor Research
For each important competitor record:
- geography;
- target customer;
- product;
- pricing;
- strengths;
- complaints and weaknesses;
- integrations;
- customer feedback;
- evidence sources.

Also track:
- market size;
- trends;
- opportunity gaps;
- customer reviews;
- search visibility.
