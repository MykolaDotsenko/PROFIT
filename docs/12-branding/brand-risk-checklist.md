# Brand Risk Checklist

Use before approving a name, slogan or major visual identity.

## Name Risk
- [ ] Trademark search completed in priority markets.
- [ ] Main domains checked.
- [ ] Social handles checked where relevant.
- [ ] Search-engine ambiguity checked.
- [ ] Competitor similarity checked.
- [ ] Pronunciation checked.
- [ ] Spelling checked.
- [ ] Negative meanings checked in target languages.
- [ ] Agriculture-industry confusion checked.
- [ ] Ability to expand beyond one crop/animal type checked.

## Slogan Risk
- [ ] Claim is understandable.
- [ ] Claim is supportable.
- [ ] Translation risk checked.
- [ ] Competitor similarity checked.
- [ ] Not overly restrictive for future product expansion.

## Logo Risk
- [ ] Competitor visual similarity checked.
- [ ] Trademark/design conflicts checked where needed.
- [ ] Works at small size.
- [ ] Works in monochrome.
- [ ] Works digitally and in print.
- [ ] Accessibility checked.
