# Logo Planning

## Objective
What should the logo communicate about PROFIT?

## Requirements
- recognizable at small sizes;
- works in monochrome;
- works on light and dark backgrounds;
- scalable from app icon to signage;
- avoids generic agriculture clichés where possible;
- visually distinct from competitors;
- suitable for international use.

## Concept Directions
Document each concept separately.

For each concept record:
- idea;
- symbolism;
- strengths;
- weaknesses;
- similarity risks;
- usage examples.

## Evaluation Criteria
- recognition;
- simplicity;
- distinctiveness;
- relevance;
- scalability;
- timelessness;
- international fit.
