# Brand Strategy

## Brand Purpose
Why does PROFIT exist beyond software features?

## Audience
Who must trust and remember the brand?

## Brand Promise
What should customers consistently expect from PROFIT?

## Positioning
How should PROFIT be understood compared with alternatives?

## Brand Attributes
Choose a small number of attributes such as:
- practical;
- trustworthy;
- intelligent;
- clear;
- global;
- farmer-focused;
- financially disciplined.

## Emotional Goal
How should a farmer feel when using or seeing the brand?

## Differentiation
What should make PROFIT recognizably different from other agritech brands?

## International Requirements
Check pronunciation, meaning and cultural fit across target markets.
