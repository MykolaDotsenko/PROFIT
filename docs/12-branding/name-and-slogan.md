## Naming Criteria
Evaluate:
- easy to pronounce;
- easy to spell;
- memorable;
- internationally usable;
- relevant without being too narrow;
- suitable for a global agritech company;
- SEO/search distinctiveness;
- domain availability;
- trademark risk;
- no negative meanings in target languages.
