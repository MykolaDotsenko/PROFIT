## Naming Objective

The final master brand should be:

- easy to pronounce;
- easy to spell;
- memorable;
- internationally usable;
- suitable for a global agritech company;
- relevant without limiting the company to one crop, animal type or product module;
- distinctive in search;
- suitable for a strong domain strategy;
- defensible from a trademark perspective;
- free of problematic meanings in priority languages and markets.
