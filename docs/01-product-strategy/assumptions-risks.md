# Assumptions and Risks

## Product Assumptions
What must be true for users to receive value?

| Assumption | Evidence | Risk if Wrong | How to Test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Market Assumptions
Examples:
- target farms will pay;
- buyer has budget authority;
- existing alternatives are insufficient;
- market is large enough.

## Data Assumptions
Examples:
- required farm data exists;
- users can provide it;
- integrations are technically and legally possible;
- data quality is sufficient.

## Delivery Risks
Examples:
- technical complexity;
- integration dependency;
- adoption friction;
- onboarding cost;
- support burden.

## Risk Levels
Use Low / Medium / High based on probability and impact.

## Rule
High-impact assumptions with weak evidence should be tested before large engineering investment.
