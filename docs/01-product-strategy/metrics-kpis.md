# Product Metrics and KPIs

## North Star
Define the single best measure that represents recurring customer value.

## Farmer Outcome Metrics
Examples:
- profit per field / herd / production unit;
- cost reduction;
- margin improvement;
- yield or productivity improvement;
- avoided losses;
- decision time reduced.

## Product Usage Metrics
Examples:
- activation;
- weekly/monthly active farms;
- key workflow completion;
- retention;
- feature adoption;
- data completeness.

## Business Metrics
Examples:
- MRR/ARR;
- conversion;
- ARPA;
- churn;
- expansion revenue;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- payback period.

## Quality Metrics
Examples:
- calculation accuracy;
- failed imports;
- error rate;
- support tickets;
- uptime;
- latency.

## Metric Definition Template
For every KPI define:
- name;
- business meaning;
- formula;
- data source;
- unit;
- owner;
- reporting frequency;
- target;
- known limitations.
