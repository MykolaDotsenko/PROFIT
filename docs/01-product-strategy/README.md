# Product Planning

This folder is the planning hub for PROFIT.

Use the documents in this order:

1. `product-vision.md` — why PROFIT exists and the long-term direction.
2. `target-customers.md` — who we build for and which farm segments matter first.
3. `personas-jtbd/` — key user roles and Jobs To Be Done.
4. `value-proposition.md` — why customers should use and pay for PROFIT.
5. `product-principles.md` — rules used to make product decisions.
6. `problem-priorities.md` — which farmer problems matter most.
7. `mvp-scope.md` — what is in and out of the MVP.
8. `prioritization-framework.md` — how features and problems are ranked.
9. `roadmap/` — what we plan to deliver and in what order.
10. `metrics-kpis.md` — how product success is measured.
11. `assumptions-risks.md` — what could make the plan wrong.
12. `research-plan.md` — what must be validated with farmers and data.
13. `dependencies.md` — important external and internal dependencies.
14. `release-plan.md` — how product increments move toward production.
15. `product-planning-checklist.md` — final planning quality check.

## Rule

Do not start building a major feature only because it sounds useful.

A major feature should have:
- a documented farmer problem;
- a target user;
- expected business value;
- a measurable outcome;
- priority reasoning;
- an MVP scope;
- clear requirements before implementation.
