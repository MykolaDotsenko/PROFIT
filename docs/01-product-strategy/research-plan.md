# Product Research Plan

## Research Goal
What decision should this research help us make?

## Questions
What do we need to learn?

## Target Participants
Farm type, role, size, geography and experience.

## Methods
Choose only what is useful:
- farmer interviews;
- workflow observation;
- existing data analysis;
- survey;
- prototype test;
- usability test;
- competitor/review research;
- pricing interview;
- pilot.

## Evidence to Capture
Record:
- source;
- date;
- farm/user context;
- exact problem/workflow;
- frequency;
- economic consequence;
- current workaround;
- quotes where useful;
- confidence.

## Decision Criteria
What result would:
- confirm the hypothesis;
- weaken it;
- invalidate it?

## Output
Research findings should update farmer problems, personas/JTBD, priorities, MVP or roadmap — not remain isolated notes.
