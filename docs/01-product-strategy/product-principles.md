# Product Principles

Use these principles when product decisions conflict.

## 1. Profit before feature count
A feature should improve a measurable farm outcome, decision or workflow.

## 2. Farmer problem before solution
Start from evidence of a real problem, not from technology.

## 3. Simple before powerful
The first useful workflow should be understandable without training.

## 4. Verified numbers
Financial and production calculations must be explainable and auditable.

## 5. One source of truth
Avoid duplicate data and conflicting calculations.

## 6. Actionable intelligence
Analytics should help the user decide or act, not only display data.

## 7. Modular growth
New modules should connect to a shared farm, economic and operational model.

## 8. Trust and data control
Users must understand where data comes from and who can access it.

## Exceptions
Document major exceptions in the decision log.
