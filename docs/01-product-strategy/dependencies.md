# Product Dependencies

Track dependencies that can block or change the product plan.

## External Dependencies
Examples:
- weather providers;
- satellite data;
- market-price data;
- government datasets;
- accounting integrations;
- machinery/IoT integrations;
- payment providers.

## Internal Dependencies
Examples:
- shared farm data model;
- authentication/permissions;
- economic calculation engine;
- design system;
- analytics;
- infrastructure.

## Dependency Table

| Dependency | Needed By | Owner | Risk | Required Date | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rule
A roadmap item is not ready for commitment if a critical dependency has no owner or fallback.
