# UX and Design

PROFIT UX is organised around real farmer decisions and workflows rather than isolated screens.

The production-oriented design-system contract lives in **[design-system/README.md](design-system/README.md)**.

## Workflow specification

For each important workflow document:

1. **User** — who performs it?
2. **Trigger** — what starts it?
3. **Goal** — what must be achieved?
4. **Evidence** — what shows this problem/workflow matters?
5. **Steps** — start to finish.
6. **Data** — required or produced.
7. **Decisions** — choices the user makes.
8. **Errors / Edge Cases** — what can go wrong?
9. **Uncertainty** — what is observed, calculated, estimated, modelled or missing?
10. **Success State** — what completion looks like.
11. **Measurement** — how task success/outcome will be evaluated.
12. **Related Problem** — link the farmer problem.
13. **Related Requirements** — link the specification.

## UX layers

### Farmer

What happened? Does it matter? What is the economic implication? What should I do?

### Manager

Costs, operations, comparison, scenarios, benchmarks and variance.

### Expert

Source/raw data, assumptions, confidence, model/methodology and audit trail.

Do not force all three levels into one default dashboard.

## AI UX

Prefer contextual actions such as Explain this, Why did this change?, Compare scenarios, Find anomalies, Show assumptions and Show evidence.

Do not use generic AI chat as a substitute for a clear workflow.
