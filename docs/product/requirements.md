# Requirements

Use this template for a major feature or module.

## Problem
Link the farmer problem.

## Target User
Who uses it? Who buys or approves it?

## Desired Outcome
What should improve?

## Scope
What is included?

## Out of Scope
What is excluded?

## User Flow
Describe the end-to-end workflow.

## Functional Requirements
What must the system do?

## Business Rules
Calculations, validations, statuses and important rules.

## Data and Integrations
What data and integrations are required?

## Permissions
Who can view, create, edit, approve or delete?

## Acceptance Criteria
Use clear, testable conditions.

## Metrics
How will success be measured?

## Risks / Open Questions
What is unresolved?
