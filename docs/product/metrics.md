# Product Metrics

## Farmer Outcomes
Examples:
- profit per field / herd / production unit;
- cost reduction;
- margin improvement;
- productivity improvement;
- avoided losses;
- decision time reduced.

## Product Usage
Activation, workflow completion, retention, adoption and data completeness.

## Business
MRR/ARR, conversion, ARPA, churn, expansion, CAC, LTV and payback.

## Quality
Calculation accuracy, failed imports, errors, support tickets, uptime and latency.

For every KPI define its formula, source, unit, owner, target and limitations.
