# Product Planning

## Discovery
For major work:
1. identify the farmer problem;
2. understand the user and current workflow;
3. estimate frequency and economic impact;
4. collect evidence;
5. define the desired outcome;
6. explore solutions;
7. test risky assumptions;
8. define the smallest useful solution.

## Prioritization

| Problem | Segment | Frequency | Economic Impact | Evidence | PROFIT Fit | Priority |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Evidence: Hypothesis / Observed / Repeated / Validated.

## Business Case
Document:
- opportunity;
- target segment;
- expected farmer value;
- expected PROFIT business value;
- cost;
- alternatives;
- decision.

## Risks and Assumptions

| Assumption / Risk | Evidence | Impact if Wrong | Test / Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Ready for Implementation
Before major implementation confirm:
- farmer problem is documented;
- target user is clear;
- evidence exists;
- expected outcome is measurable;
- MVP scope is clear;
- priority is justified;
- requirements are ready;
- success KPI is defined.
